Questions? Answered.

What is Squeeze?
Squeeze is a minimalist local browser extension designed specifically for developers using Claude.ai. It integrates natively into the chat text area, stripping polite greetings, redundant fillers, and verbose technical phrases locally before they are sent to Anthropic.
Does my prompt data go to any external server?
No. Squeeze operates 100% offline and locally inside your browser sandbox. It evaluates your text via plain heuristic script strings on your client device and performs the text replacement directly inside the DOM before the submit event triggers. No analytical logs or prompts are transmitted to us.
Does it interfere with Claude's Prompt Caching?
No, it actually improves it! Anthropics's Claude prompt caching triggers when large chunks of prefix text remain perfectly identical across turns. Standard greeting words like "Hi Claude", conversational references, or arbitrary politeness vary from turn to turn, destroying cache matches. Squeeze normalizes prompts, resulting in clean, consistent prefixes that are highly cache-safe.
What are the optimization profiles?
Squeeze: Highly aggressive telegraphic mode. Strips articles ("the", "a"), auxiliary verbs, and shortens technical words ("function" → "fn", "database" → "DB").
Balanced (Default): Strips opening greetings ("hello Claude", "hope you're doing well") and standard filler phrases ("in order to").
Polish: Cleans spaces, normalizes list layout syntax, and strips unnecessary empty blocks.
